Who We Are

A community where no one rises alone.

Black Women Rising is a community of women who believe no one has to rise alone. We were built on a simple truth: the right person, at the right moment, can change the entire course of a career. So we made a place where that kind of moment happens often, not by chance, but by design.

Our Story

“People heal and rebuild fastest when they are not alone.”

Nneka Obiekwe, Founder

A newsletter that keeps you close to what's happening. A network built on real introductions, not cold outreach. Curated spaces where relationships deepen into something lasting.

What We Do

We connect women to people, rooms, and opportunities.

However you find us, you'll find a community that shows up — whether that's through a weekly newsletter, a warm introduction, or a room built for the relationships that move careers forward.

Founder

Nneka Obiekwe

Nneka spent years as a go-to connector for friends job-hunting — until her network was tapped out and she noticed most of the people asking were Black women who needed community as much as a referral. In September 2025, she started a WhatsApp chat and posted the link on Threads. Within 24 hours, more than 500 people had joined.

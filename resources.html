Workforce Resource Guide

Free job matching, training, and hiring programs available across the country, plus examples of what states and cities have set up on their own.

Prepared by Nneka Obiekwe, Nobi Consulting · Updated October 23, 2026

Anywhere in the U.S. State programs City and regional
Part 1

Anywhere in the U.S.

Start here if someone isn’t sure where to go. These work in every state, and most are free.

American Job Centers

In person, free

Nearly 2,300 local centers funded by the U.S. Department of Labor. CareerOneStop is the website that helps you find the closest one.

  • Job search help, training referrals, and unemployment questions
  • Free computers, printers, and phones for your search
  • Resume and basic computer workshops at many locations
  • Extra programs for veterans, youth, and people with disabilities
Toll free: 1-877-872-5627 (1-877-US2-JOBS)
Find a center near you

Civic Match

Government jobs

A free job platform for state and local government roles, run by the nonprofit Work for America. It started for displaced federal workers and is now open to anyone interested in public service.

  • 440 state and local government partners in 49 states
  • About 14,000 users and 500+ people hired so far
  • About 500 new jobs posted each month, many in data, cybersecurity, and AI
  • Governments can post roles for free too
Join Civic Match

Apprenticeship.gov

Earn while you learn

The Department of Labor’s search for open apprenticeships. You search by career or location and apply straight to the employer.

  • Paid, on-the-job training with a credential at the end
  • Listings show whether the role or employer is registered
  • A partner finder shows who is taking new apprentices
Search apprenticeships

Per Scholas

Tech training

A national nonprofit with tuition-free tech training and career coaching. It has about 29 campuses plus a remote option you can take from anywhere in the country.

  • No-cost immersive training with career coaching
  • Low-cost upskilling for mid-career professionals
  • Remote campus for people with no location nearby
See locations

Stellarworx

No degree needed

A job matching platform from Opportunity@Work for people who built skills through work, the military, community college, or bootcamps instead of a four-year degree.

  • Matches candidates to employers on skills, not degrees
  • Open nationwide, strongest in DC, the Bay Area, Los Angeles, and Houston
  • Employers like Accenture, Amazon, and Workday have hired through it
Visit stellarworx.org
Part 2

State programs

Some states built new career platforms. Others opened fast-track hiring for workers affected by federal cuts. Many of these launched in 2025, so check the site for what’s still running.

Virginia Has Jobs

Virginia

The state’s job and training hub, run by Virginia Works. It launched for displaced federal workers and later added an AI Career Launch Pad built with Google.

  • Job listings in tech, healthcare, manufacturing, and more
  • Unemployment, training, and community college info in one place
  • AI courses and Google Career Certificates through the Launch Pad
  • 10,000 no-cost Google scholarships handed out by the state
Visit virginiahasjobs.com

Maryland Public Servants Resource Website

Maryland

The governor’s site for federal workers, contractors, and grantees affected by federal cuts.

  • Unemployment insurance and health coverage information
  • Job search and career support
  • Legal resources and a guide to federal employment rules
Visit the Maryland site

Pennsylvania fast-track hiring

Pennsylvania

A 2025 executive order speeds up state hiring of former federal workers into funded vacancies. The state posts its roles on Civic Match.

  • 800+ former federal workers hired by the state in a year
  • Roles in public safety, human services, health care, and more
  • Hundreds of state vacancies listed at any given time
Find PA roles on Civic Match

New York: You’re Hired

New York

A state recruitment campaign that invites former federal workers to apply for New York government jobs.

  • Aimed at filling 7,000+ open state positions
  • Roles from engineers and lawyers to first responders and mental health staff
  • All openings are listed on StateJobsNY
Visit ny.gov/wewantyou

My Career NJ

New Jersey

New Jersey’s online career hub with two free tools for job seekers and career changers.

  • Career Navigator suggests jobs and paths based on your skills and history
  • Training Explorer compares programs, providers, and tuition help
  • Thousands of online upskilling classes
Visit My Career NJ

LAUNCH

Arkansas

Arkansas’s free employment portal that puts job seekers, employers, and state workforce services in one place.

  • AI matching based on skills, plus suggested training
  • Apply for several state services at once
  • Connected to the state’s unemployment system
Visit LAUNCH

Free Google AI training

Massachusetts

A 2026 partnership between the state and Google that gives every Massachusetts resident free access to Google certificates.

  • Google AI Professional Certificate
  • Career Certificates in cybersecurity, IT support, project management, UX, and digital marketing
  • Open to small business owners too
Get the certificates

Hawaii expedited hiring

Hawaii

An executive order that speeds up state agency hiring for workers affected by federal firings or budget cuts.

  • Faster path into Hawaii state government roles
  • Check the governor’s announcement for how to apply
Read the announcement
Part 3

City and regional

Local efforts that look a lot like Talent Capital: one region or city pooling jobs, training, and support in one place.

Talent Capital

DC · MD · VA

The model for this guide. A free regional platform from the Metropolitan Washington Council of Governments, built by BuildWithin.

  • 83,000+ active users and 65,000 full-time jobs
  • AI matching, training, and in-person hiring events
Visit talentcapital.ai

Ready to Work

San Antonio, TX

A city program paid for by a voter-approved sales tax, with funding planned through 2029. It serves underemployed residents and has stepped in for people affected by federal layoffs.

  • Training from short certificates up to degree programs
  • Job placement plus support like rental assistance
  • Thousands placed in jobs and enrolled in training
Workforce Solutions Alamo hotline: 210-224-4357
How to apply

RVA Builds

Richmond, VA

A skilled trades program for Richmond Public Schools students, run by Richmond Ed Fund with $8 million from Bloomberg Philanthropies. BuildWithin, the company behind Talent Capital, is the tech partner.

  • Paid work experience in electrical, HVAC, welding, and construction
  • At least $23 an hour in total pay after graduation
  • Goal of 500 students placed by the 2028–2029 school year
Read more

YSU Talent Marketplace

Northeast Ohio

Youngstown State University’s regional program with Opportunity@Work that connects local workers without four-year degrees to local employers.

  • Skills-based matching for workers in the region
  • Built on the same approach as Stellarworx
Visit the marketplace

Where to send someone

Not sure where to start: their nearest American Job Center
Former federal worker: Civic Match, then their state’s program
Wants a government job: Civic Match
Wants paid training: Apprenticeship.gov
Wants to move into tech: Per Scholas or their state’s Google certificate program
No four-year degree: Stellarworx
